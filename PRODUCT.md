# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two confirmed audiences, both arriving with judgment already in progress:

- **Peers and collaborators** — other developers, designers, security people and community
  organisers. They assess credibility fast, on technical specifics, and are looking for
  someone worth working alongside.
- **Freelance and project clients** — evaluating whether Kinshuk can deliver a specific
  piece of work before deciding to reach out.

Neither audience is a recruiter scanning a resume. Both can tell the difference between
a claim and evidence, and both have seen the generic developer portfolio many times.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that establishes Kinshuk as a multidisciplinary technologist who
combines technical problem-solving with creative execution — software and web development,
cybersecurity learning, automation, graphic design, VFX and motion, and community
leadership.

**Success is belief, not conversion.** A visitor who reaches the bottom of the page should
come away convinced the person is credible. Contact is available and real, but it is not
the measure of the page working. This makes the site standing proof rather than a funnel,
and it means the impression left by specificity, restraint and finish outranks any CTA
metric.

## Positioning

The combination is the position: security instinct, engineering practice and visual craft
in one person, presented as one coherent identity rather than three unrelated skill lists.
Central message: **technology × creativity**.

What a neighboring portfolio could not truthfully copy is the honesty posture — the site
labels its own unfinished areas rather than dressing them up, which is itself the argument
for the person's judgment.

## Operating Context

- Single-page primary surface with per-project detail routes.
- Evaluated in a browser, usually in one sitting, often on mobile, frequently from a link
  shared in a Discord or DM rather than from search.
- Content is authored by editing typed data files, not a CMS. Whoever updates this site is
  Kinshuk, in an editor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed capabilities represented:** software and web development, Python/JavaScript
scripting and automation, cybersecurity study (labs, Linux, networking, web security,
OSINT, privacy), graphic design, VFX/motion/video editing, competitive gaming, community
and team leadership.

**Hard constraints on all future work:**

- **Never invent achievements, projects, employers, certifications, clients, statistics,
  testimonials, security findings, awards, or employment history.** Absence is stated;
  it is never filled.
- **Skill levels are experience labels, never percentages.** Percentages imply a precision
  nobody has.
- **All cybersecurity content stays ethical** — authorised testing, owned hardware, labs,
  CTFs, defensive work, research and responsible disclosure only. No unauthorised access,
  no exploitation instructions, no implied illegality.
- **No claim of message delivery without a real backend.** There is currently none.

**Explicitly undecided:** contact email address, physical location, production domain,
real social handles (GitHub, LinkedIn, Discord, X), and the final inventory of real
projects. These are recorded as placeholders in `/data` and must not be guessed.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Kinshuk. Wordmark set in editorial uppercase.
- **Positioning line:** Multidisciplinary Developer • Cybersecurity Enthusiast • Creative
  Technologist.
- **Primary tagline:** "Built by mistakes. Refined by experience."
- **Supporting lines**, used sparingly and never all at once: "Technology meets
  creativity." · "Creating. Breaking. Rebuilding." · "Learning by building." · "The person
  changed. The mindset evolved."
- **Personality:** curious, technical, creative, disciplined, evolving.
- **Voice:** confident, mature, grounded, intelligent, personal, slightly mysterious.
  Capable without arrogant.
- **Voice exclusions (binding):** no corporate filler, no "passionate professional" or
  "results-driven individual", no empty motivational statements, no fake confidence, no
  dark-hacker theatrics, no excessive self-praise.

## Evidence on Hand

**Real:** the portfolio codebase itself — Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript,
Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Lucide; content separated into typed data files under
`/data`; every route statically prerendered.

**Absent, and not to be fabricated:**

- No project imagery, screenshots, artwork, reels or portrait photograph. `/public/*`
  holds only placement notes.
- No verified CTF participation, certifications, bug bounties, rankings or awards.
- No client work, employment history or testimonials.
- No published security write-ups (drafts are labelled as unpublished).
- No live contact address, repository links or social destinations.

Placeholder entries carry `placeholder: true` in `/data` and render a visible badge
wherever they appear. Removing that flag is a claim that the entry is real.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** A small finished thing shown beats a large thing described.
2. **Honesty is the differentiator, not a caveat.** Labelled scaffolding demonstrates the
   judgment the site is arguing for; hidden scaffolding destroys it.
3. **Credibility lives in specifics.** Named constraints, real trade-offs and actual
   process read as competence. Adjectives do not.
4. **The unfinished state is permanent, so design it properly.** This site ships before the
   work catalogue is complete, and will spend a long time partly populated. Placeholder,
   empty and in-progress states are first-class design surfaces, not temporary ugliness.
5. **Ethics is a product constraint.** The security posture is part of what the site
   asserts about the person; it is never softened for effect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Confirmed requirements from the brief, binding on all future work: semantic HTML,
full keyboard operability, screen-reader support, visible focus indicators, labelled
controls and forms, alt text on real imagery, sufficient colour contrast, and honoured
`prefers-reduced-motion` (parallax, page transitions and background motion disabled).
Every layout must remain legible and complete with animation switched off.
